--- Implementation ---
- MutatingLaneConfig model (PlanConfig.cs): Id, Kind, Name, Prompt, StageTrigger, TimeoutMinutes, Enabled, Agent, MergeGates
- PlanConfig.MutatingLanes: List<MutatingLaneConfig> (defaults empty)
- Git.cs: WorktreeAdd, WorktreeRemove, MergeBranch, DeleteBranch helpers
- MutatingLaneRunner.cs: creates worktree on scratch branch, runs agent, staging worktree merge gate, fast-forward merge on pass
- Events: MutatingLaneStarted, MutatingLaneFinished, MergeGateVerdict
- Merge gate: creates staging worktree from base, merges scratch branch, runs gates (lane-specific or plan-level), green=accept red=reject
- Worktree cleanup: lane worktree removed, scratch branch deleted (best-effort)
