Design decisions:
- Backward compatible: all legacy action strings ("retry", "resume", "skip", "human")
  are parsed by TryParseAction. Old templates produce valid AdvisorAction values.
- Remediation is configurable but optional — no RemediationScript means ApplyFix
  logs a warning and continues as retry.
- BlockRetry is stronger than NeedsHuman — it also exhausts the attempt budget so
  the orchestrator loop won't auto-retry even after a human resumes.
