Read this before the first session: the work here is four followup rows that have been open since
the B/F-series audits and were re-verified as still-real during the 2026-07-28 triage. Each is
small, mechanical and independently gated by the full battery. That is deliberate — **W5.2 is a
test of the engine, not of the agent.** The work must be real enough that gates can genuinely fail,
and small enough that a cheap-tier model finishing it says something about the loop rather than
about the model.

Everything the agent needs is in the stage `notes` of the plan, down to file:line. The tracker rows
below are the contract: claim through `conductor task --done <id> -c <sha> -e "<evidence>"` and
nothing else — do not hand-edit the Status column, it is a generated view of the work graph.

**What "pass" means for this run** (from the brief, `docs/CONDUCTOR-WORKGRAPH.md:180`): started
once, driven **unattended** to a `RunFinished` event, budget cap set, watchdog armed but idle, and
**zero owner interventions**. Rescuing it mid-run means it did not pass — stop it, fix what bled,
and start again. That is the brief's "fix what bleeds; re-run until clean".
