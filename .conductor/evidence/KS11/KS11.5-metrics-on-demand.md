## 7. The goldens that moved, and why (commit `7d461bb`)

Six moved by exactly one line — the ask-line grew from `/status, /tasks, /daily, /evidence` to
`/status, /tasks, /daily, /progress, /evidence, /money, /tokens`, because the list is derived from
`SurfaceCommand.Implemented` and the three handlers landed. The seventh, `testdata/ks11/cmd-daily.txt`,
moved because KS11.5's exit *is* the digest recomposed; it pinned the pre-seam rendering
(`Cost: $42.0000`, no cap, no progress, no tokens), and now pins the grammar's. The other fourteen
KS11.1 seam goldens are untouched, so the byte-identical seam claim is unaffected.

## 8. Honest caveats

* A lane under a dollar prints as `$0.5000`. That is `MoneyLine.Usd`'s existing rule (four decimals
  below a dollar, so a real $0.004 session does not render as `$0.00`), applied unchanged — not a new
  format decision.
* The digest prints a still-running session's outcome as the store records it (`Unknown`). Left as
  recorded rather than relabelled "in flight", because a crashed session records the same way and the
  relabel would be a guess.
* The cross-check is against `conductor money`'s *computation*, in-process, over a rig database — not
  against the verb's rendered table. The verb's own renderer (`MoneyCommand.Render`) formats the same
  `MoneyRun`; nothing in this checkpoint touches it.
