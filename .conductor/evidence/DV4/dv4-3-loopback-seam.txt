delivered as its text plus a visible "not attached - it is at <path>" line
(src/Conductor.Core/Integrations/TelegramCourierSource.cs:179), the shape TelegramService
already uses for an artifact over the size limit. The wire already carries the path and the
daemon is on the same machine, so the fix is small once TelegramService's multipart sender
is extracted from _composer.Stamp / BasePayload / RememberAnchor.

/status was proven through the roll-up string it prints (MessageComposer.Views.cs:67 formats
ChannelHealthProbe.SummaryLine verbatim), not by driving a bot - that needs a live run with a
messenger, which no rig here may have.
