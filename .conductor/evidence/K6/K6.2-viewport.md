## The measurement, before and after

The K6.1 measurement (ADR, "Context"): `m.reportScroll++` at `tab_report.go:46` was unbounded in
`Update`; the only clamp was a local copy at `tab_report.go:83` inside a value-receiver `View` that
could never be written back. At 120x30 the Report body stopped changing at offset 12, **400 `down`
presses left the field at 400, and 389 `up` presses were needed before one line moved.**

After: the offset lives in `viewport.Model`, whose `SetYOffset` clamps on every mutation
(`viewport.go` — `clamp(n, 0, m.maxYOffset())`), so it cannot leave the body at all.

`TestScrollingPastTheEndCostsExactlyOneKeyToComeBack` re-runs that measurement on both surfaces and
fails with the real count if it is ever more than one. It asserts the COUNT and not "the pane is not
blank", because a renderer-side clamp already passed the blank test — which is exactly how this
shipped to the owner.

**Frames 2 and 3 of `K6.2-frames.txt` are that case end to end:** Report after 400 `j` presses reads
`100%` at the foot; one `↑` later it reads `97%` and the body has moved.

## Two deviations from the ADR, both measured

The ADR is a decision record, not a spec to follow off a cliff; where it was measurably wrong the
code does the right thing and says so at the site.

1. **`f` is not bound to page-down.** The ADR offered `f` as an alias on the belief that it "was never
   bound". It is: `tab_agent.go:36` binds `f` to fold-tools and `cmdbar.go` documents it in the
   Actions row with no tab qualifier. One letter meaning two things in one legend is the drift this
   ADR exists to kill. Page-down is `pgdown` alone (`panescroll.go`).
2. **The key is spelled `pgdown`.** bubbletea v2 spells it that way (`key.go:30`, `uv.KeyPgDown`);
   the ADR's `pgdn` is the same key.

Everything else in the ADR's table is bound exactly as written: `↓`/`j`, `↑`, `d`/`u`, `pgdown`,
`pgup`, `end`/`G`, `home` — and **no `k`**, which opens Knowledge everywhere and always will.

## The golden diff is the proof the render did not change

Five frames moved, and **not one of them is a body row** — at any offset, including the frame
scrolled forty presses past the end:

| golden | the one line that changed |
| --- | --- |
| `help` | the Actions row now names the real scroll set |
| `report`, `report_scrolled`, `report_bottom` | `↑↓ scroll (6/13)` → `↑↓ d/u G/home scroll 46%` |
| `knowledge_note` | the note prompt sits at the foot of the pane, not under short content |

That is what says `viewport.View()` renders the same bytes the hand-rolled slice did once
`frameContent`'s `.Width`/`.Height` have run. K6.3 and K6.4 can move the remaining panes onto a
viewport without fearing body-row golden churn.
