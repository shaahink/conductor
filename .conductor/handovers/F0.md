- **FU-F0-4**: 3× `.GetAwaiter().GetResult()` at Spectre.Cli sync boundaries (`Commands.cs:80, :91, :1424`). Currently safe (no `SynchronizationContext` in console apps) but fragile. Spectre.Cli supports `async Task<int> ExecuteAsync` — convert when the next phase touches Commands.cs.

## Risks for F1 (run.db)

1. **SQLite introduces file-locking**: The `run.db` write path must coexist with `events.jsonl` and `state.json` writers. Verify no file-lock contention under concurrent drain.
2. **Tracker-as-view must be byte-stable**: Generated `TRACKER.md` must be deterministic (no timestamps, no GUIDs) or the normalizer will produce noise commits. F0's killed heartbeat commit machinery was the old source of noise — the new generator must not re-introduce it.
3. **CT threading**: Continue the pattern established here — every new async method should accept and respect `CancellationToken`.
