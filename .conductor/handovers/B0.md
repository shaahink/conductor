- **Warnings-as-errors is the quality gate, and it is real.** `TreatWarningsAsErrors=true` +
  `CodeAnalysisTreatWarningsAsErrors=true` + `EnforceCodeStyleInBuild=true` + `AnalysisLevel=latest`.
  Proof the analyzers actually run: the diff contains their forced fixes — `Program.cs` →
  `RunAsync().ConfigureAwait(false)` (MA0042/MA0004), `AgentSession` → `StringComparison.Ordinal`
  (MA0074), `Commands` CTS → `using` (CA2000), `PromptBuilder` → `ArgumentException(..., nameof(name))`
  (MA0015), `LiveDashboard` fire-and-forget wrapped so failures surface (MA0134), and 4× `object` →
  `System.Threading.Lock` (MA0158). All are correct, behaviour-preserving improvements.
- **Curated ruleset, honestly documented.** `.editorconfig` keeps the async/threading-correctness
  theme at `error` (MA0004/MA0040/MA0042/MA0079/CA2007/CA2016/CA1849/MA0074). Relaxations are

  are silent. `Conductor.slnx` verified referencing both projects (R0.5).
- **Self-plan is loadable by the STABLE driver** via the temp-repo lock workaround:
  `docs/baton/evidence/B0.3-gate.txt` (dry-run) and `B0.4-gate.txt` (`--once` smoke).

  genuine A6 ("ship without launch") bug: the script emitted opencode-json flat at root, but the

- **Baseline audit is real, not filler.** `docs/baton/audits/B0-baseline.md` — 40+ file:line
