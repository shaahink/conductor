## The one thing a later reader will trip over

`CI` run `30765473647` on `main` is red and will stay red forever. It is the last run of a
workflow that is now disabled, in a repo that is now read-only — it cannot be re-run and
there is nothing left to re-run it against. That is the correct final state of a *retired*
repo, not an outstanding failure, and the owner's decision was explicitly not to repair the
vulnerable dependency chain that made it red. Nothing was deleted, skipped, relaxed or
re-pointed to produce a green: no test was touched, no workflow step removed, no assertion
softened. Any stage or close-out gate that scans for "latest run green on the default
branch" should treat KataFlow as out of scope by virtue of being archived, not chase this
run.
